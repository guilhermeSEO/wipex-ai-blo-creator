## 4. BRIEF-SPECIFIC ADJUDICATION

The brief asks for **"comparison intent + cost-per-use"** and the CTA
**"Reduce Table Turnover Time"**. Three judgement calls:

### 4.1 Comparison intent → ✅ ALLOWED, but only as *cost/format* comparison
The brief's "comparison" must be served by comparing **cost per use, pack format and
labor time** — all of which are our own list-price facts and restaurant-operations
statistics from cited third-party sources. It must **not** become a comparison of
cleaning performance against any other wipe. Concretely:

- ✅ "A 400-count bucket at $36.99 works out to about 9¢ per wipe."
- ✅ "Paper towels run about 5¢ a sheet *and* need a separate spray." (third-party figure, cited)
- ❌ "Cleans better than traditional disinfecting wipes."
- ❌ "Outperforms paper towels."

### 4.2 Cost-per-use numbers → ✅ ALLOWED (with one guardrail)
Cost maths are arithmetic on **our own prices** (WX01126TN $36.99/400ct ≈ $0.0925 per wipe),
not a performance claim and not a benchmark. Guardrail: no cost figure may sit **beside a
cleaning-performance claim, a percentage or the word "safe"** (Part 3 adjacency rule).
Building the cost table as a *pricing* table keeps it clean.

### 4.3 CTA "Reduce Table Turnover Time" → 🟡 MEDIUM FINDING — needs Dean's nod
This is an **outcome claim about operational time**. It is not Tier 1 or Tier 2 (it is not
a cleaning-performance claim), and it is the language the market is actively searching —
but it implies a measurable time saving that Wipex has not tested for these SKUs.
**Recommendation:** keep the CTA as an *objective/benefit statement*, never as a measured
result. Approved-adjacent framing:

- ✅ "Reduce Table Turnover Time" (imperative objective, no measured claim)
- ✅ "Designed around faster table resets"
- ❌ "Cuts table reset time by 30%"
- ❌ "Reduce table turnover time — proven"

Flag to Dean as MEDIUM for a logged decision at the next Filter revision.
