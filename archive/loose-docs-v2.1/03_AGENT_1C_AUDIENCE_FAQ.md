## 2. FAQ OUTLINE (5 questions)

Format: **Question | Pain trigger | Product benefit | Wipex claim (compliance-safe) | Bridge CTA**

**Q1. "What is a good table turnover time — and how fast should a reset be?"**
- Pain: they don't know if their number is bad.
- Benefit: sets the benchmark that makes fast resets measurable.
- Claim: external only, cited — targets of **under 90 seconds** for a reset (Lavu, Mar 2026);
  a **3–5 minute** full reset target for casual dining (FlipMenu, Mar 2026).
- Bridge: *"Once the reset is the bottleneck, the wipe is the last step — see the cost-per-table table."* → **Reduce Table Turnover Time**

**Q2. "How much does it cost per use to clean a table?"**
- Pain: disposables spend is invisible per table.
- Benefit: breaks cleaning down to cents per table per turn.
- Claim: **cost arithmetic on Wipex list prices** (WX01126TN $36.99 ÷ 400 wipes ≈ $0.09/wipe);
  third-party paper-towel figure (~5¢/sheet, The Zero Store, 2026) for contrast. **No performance comparison.**
- Bridge: *"Run your own table count through the calculator."* → Add to cart

**Q3. "Do pre-moistened table wipes save labor vs paper towels and a spray?"**
- Pain: every reset that needs two trips costs seconds × covers × days.
- Benefit: one-pass reset — wipe is the cleaner and the applicator.
- Claim: Tier 3 only — *"a proprietary formulation designed for tables, counters and food-service surfaces"*
  + qualitative ("cuts through spills and residue"). **No "saves X seconds" claim.**
- Bridge: *"See how many resets a 400-count bucket covers."*

**Q4. "Will a scented wipe bother guests? Is there a fragrance-free option?"**
- Pain: guest comfort, sensitive diners, bars vs dining rooms.
- Benefit: two SKUs for two rooms.
- Claim: Autumn = cinnamon & clove oils (seasonal, Q4); Unscented = fragrance-free for
  sensitive environments. Neither may be called "safe" for surfaces; "safe on sensitive skin"
  (S3) is a **skin** designation only.
- Bridge: *"Choose the format for your room."* → product links (Autumn / Unscented)

**Q5. "What should I look for in food-service table wipes?"**
- Pain: afraid of buying something a health inspector or guest notices.
- Benefit: a short buying checklist.
- Claim: Autumn — **"NSF-certified"** as the certificate states + **"suitable for food service
  environments"** (exact approved phrase). Unscented — **no NSF, no food-safe**; plant-based
  viscose cloth; TÜV cloth-compostability statement with the mandatory disclaimer. **No "food-safe".**
- Bridge: *"Print the checklist for your next order."* → Add to cart
