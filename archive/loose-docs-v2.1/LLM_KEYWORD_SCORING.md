# NICHE SPECIFICITY + LLM KEYWORD SCORING MATRIX

**Why specific keywords beat broad keywords for LLMs + Blog Performance:**

The more specific your keyword, the easier it is for:
1. **Search engines** to understand your content (semantic clarity)
2. **LLMs** to cite your blog (entity recognition + context)
3. **Your audience** to find exactly what they need (intent match)
4. **Competitors** to ignore you (niche moat)

## LLM KEYWORD SCORING SYSTEM

**When Agent 1A recommends a keyword, it scores it across 5 dimensions:**

### Scoring Framework (Each dimension: 1-5)

```
1. SEMANTIC CLARITY (How easily LLMs understand your topic)
   
   Score 1: "cleaning" (what product? what surface?)
   Score 2: "wipes" (which type? what use case?)
   Score 3: "yoga mat wipes" (OK, but yoga wipes could be anything)
   Score 4: "plant-based yoga mat wipes" (clear: product type + material + use)
   Score 5: "natural plant-based yoga mat wipes TURI-tested" (ultra-clear, testable)

2. ENTITY RECOGNITION (How well NLP models identify entities in your text)
   
   Score 1: "wipes" (generic entity)
   Score 2: "yoga wipes" (two entities, loose connection)
   Score 3: "yoga mat wipes" (three entities, clear relationship)
   Score 4: "plant-based yoga mat wipes" (product + material + use case)
   Score 5: "plant-based yoga mat wipes + Natural Gym Wipes brand" (product + test reference)

3. NICHE POSITIONING (How narrow/specific is your target audience)
   
   Score 1: Broad ("anyone cleaning") = 500K+ searches
   Score 2: Medium ("fitness people") = 50K+ searches
   Score 3: Narrow ("studio owners") = 5K+ searches
   Score 4: Very Narrow ("boutique yoga studios in US") = 500-2K searches
   Score 5: Ultra-Narrow ("boutique yoga studios, eco-conscious, $50K+ budget") = 50-200 searches

4. COMPETITION MOAT (How easy is it for competitors to outrank you)
   
   Score 1: Broad terms = TOP 10 all major sites (Wirecutter, Amazon, etc.)
   Score 2: Medium terms = TOP 10 mix of authority + niche sites
   Score 3: Niche terms = Top 10 mostly niche blogs (yours can rank easily)
   Score 4: Very niche = Top 10 are specific product reviews + 1-2 blogs
   Score 5: Ultra-niche = Top 10 are ALL product pages or brand sites (easy to rank)

5. TREND MOMENTUM (Is the keyword rising, stable, or falling right now)
   
   Score 1: ↓ Falling -20% (avoid, declining interest)
   Score 2: ↓ Falling -5% (avoid, weak momentum)
   Score 3: → Stable 0-5% (OK, evergreen)
   Score 4: ↑ Rising +10-25% (good, interest growing)
   Score 5: ↑ Rising +25%+ (excellent, viral potential + calendar aligned)
```

### Overall LLM Keyword Score (Weighted)

```
FORMULA:
LLM Score = (Semantic * 0.25) + (Entity * 0.25) + (Niche * 0.15) + (Moat * 0.15) + (Trend * 0.20)

Example 1: "yoga mat wipes" (Broad)
  Semantic: 3, Entity: 3, Niche: 2, Moat: 1, Trend: 2 (stable)
  LLM Score = (3*0.25) + (3*0.25) + (2*0.15) + (1*0.15) + (2*0.20) = 2.35/5
  Recommendation: ❌ Skip (too generic, low LLM visibility)

Example 2: "plant-based yoga mat wipes" (Medium-Niche)
  Semantic: 4, Entity: 4, Niche: 3.5, Moat: 3, Trend: 4 (↑+18%)
  LLM Score = (4*0.25) + (4*0.25) + (3.5*0.15) + (3*0.15) + (4*0.20) = 3.8/5
  Recommendation: ✅ GOOD (solid LLM visibility, rising trend)

Example 3: "plant-based yoga mat wipes for boutique studios" (Niche)
  Semantic: 5, Entity: 5, Niche: 4.5, Moat: 4, Trend: 5 (↑+25%)
  LLM Score = (5*0.25) + (5*0.25) + (4.5*0.15) + (4*0.15) + (5*0.20) = 4.7/5
  Recommendation: 🟢 EXCELLENT (perfect for LLMs, high authority potential)

Example 4: "ultra-niche yoga mat wipes TURI-tested antimicrobial" (Ultra-Niche)
  Semantic: 5, Entity: 5, Niche: 5, Moat: 5, Trend: 2 (stable, no seasonal)
  LLM Score = (5*0.25) + (5*0.25) + (5*0.15) + (5*0.15) + (2*0.20) = 4.3/5
  Recommendation: 🟡 HIGH BUT RISKY (perfect semantics, but search volume may be too low)
```
