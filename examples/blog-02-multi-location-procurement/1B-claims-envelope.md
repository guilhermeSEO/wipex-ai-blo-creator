## 2. THE ENVELOPE OF THIS POST (applies to every surface, not just the body)
Scope: title · meta title · meta description · H1 · H2 · H3 · table headers · bullet labels · image captions · alt text · CTA button text · schema fields.

Blocked everywhere:
- `safe on [surface]` / `safe for all` / `won't damage` → use "gentle on / designed for / tailored for".
- Any percentage, ratio, "outperforms", "better than", "removes X% more" about cleaning performance **for Table Bussers** (P = —). Performance comparisons are only allowed on SKUs with P **and** a matching test → keep the performance surface to fitness/plant-based only.
- Germ-kill / disinfect / sanitize / antibacterial / antimicrobial — none of these SKUs is regulated; the post is not a health post.
- Benchmark brand names — **"Zogics" must not appear in any surface** (prohibited: naming a benchmark brand). Use "traditional quat-based disinfecting wipes" style wording if a category comparison is ever needed.
- bare "compostable" (use the full cloth-substrate sentence); bare "Sustainable"; "non-toxic", "chemical-free", "harmless", "kid & pet safe", "#1/best/ultimate".
- Hardware: XL Bucket Dispenser = "made with 30% recycled plastic" **only** ("planet-friendly"/"plant-based" forbidden).

Allowed:
- Tier 3 qualitative sentences ("clean", "fresh", "removes residue") on any non-BZK/non-EPA surface product.
- "suitable for food service environments" — exact phrase, **scented Table Bussers only** (NSF attaches to the scented family; does not travel to Unscented).
- Cost figures as **price units** (our own list prices) — allowed even without P, as arithmetic, because it prices the consumable, not a cleaning claim.
- Real product facts: codes N/S3/P/C/E/Eco per the row, dispenser specs, pack counts, live stock.

## 3. What the copy MAY use
Tier 3 qualitative language + the certifications in their exact form + our own recomputed price arithmetic. Material lists / instructions carry: **"If in doubt, test on a small, inconspicuous area first."**

## 4. Adjudication of the tensions this brief creates
- **Comparison intent ≠ performance comparison.** The brief wants a comparison ("wipes vs ..."); per Part 4 and the Filter, the comparison must be **cost, format, coverage or labour** — our prices and cited third-party operational statistics — never efficacy (Table Bussers has no P).
- **Cost adjacency guardrail.** A cost/price number must never sit beside a performance claim, a percentage, or the word "safe". Cost paragraphs stay cost paragraphs.
- **Arithmetic is recomputed, not inherited** (see 1C). Recompute from live prices before publish.
- **Tier-2 numbers only on P SKUs.** The 4-pack/pallet $/wipe figures are cost units (fine); do NOT place a fitness-wipe performance/premium claim next to the Table Bussers cost line (no transfer, no adjacency).
