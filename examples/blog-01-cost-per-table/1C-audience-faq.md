## 2. OBJECTIONS → HONEST ANSWERS

| Objection | Answer, and where it lives |
|---|---|
| "We already use paper towels and a spray." | Per-table arithmetic plus the second-trip problem, using cited third-party figures. Cost block. |
| "Is a pre-mixed wipe actually cheaper?" | $/wipe → $/table, recomputed from live prices. Cost block. |
| "Everyone writes 'cost per wipe'." | Correct — and that is why we write **cost per table**: the unit you're paid in. Differentiation section. |
| "Will a scented wipe bother guests?" | Autumn (cinnamon-clove) vs Unscented (fragrance-free), by room. Format chooser. |
| "Our food-service surfaces have rules." | Autumn: **"NSF-certified"** as the certificate states + **"suitable for food service environments"** (exact phrase). Unscented: **no NSF, no food-safe** — do not blur the two. FAQ. |
| "Will it damage the finish?" | Tier 3 only: "gentle on [named material]" + *"If in doubt, test on a small, inconspicuous area first."* Never "safe on". |

## 3. FAQ OUTLINE (8 questions)

Format: **Question | Pain trigger | Benefit | Claim used | Bridge CTA**

1. **What is cost per table — and how is it different from cost per wipe?**
   Pain: they track the wrong unit. Benefit: the unit that matches the P&L. Claim: cost arithmetic
   (no performance claim). CTA: "Set your cost per table".
2. **How do I calculate my own cost per table?**
   Pain: no formula. Benefit: arithmetic they can rerun. Claim: price ÷ count × wipes per reset.
   CTA: the calculator slot.
3. **What is a good table reset time?**
   Pain: no benchmark. Benefit: a target. Claim: third-party only — **under 90 seconds** (Lavu,
   checked 2026-09-23); **3–5 minutes** full reset, casual dining (FlipMenu, same date).
4. **How many wipes does one reset take?**
   Pain: guessing at consumption. Benefit: par-level planning. Claim: qualitative only —
   "one wipe per table in a single pass" (Tier 3). No measured claim.
5. **How does cost per cover relate to cost per table?**
   Pain: two numbers that sound the same. Benefit: connect spend to revenue per seat.
   Claim: third-party — casual dining averages **1.5 turns/hour** (Worldmetrics 2026).
6. **Scented or unscented across the dining room and the bar?**
   Pain: guest comfort. Benefit: two rooms, two SKUs. Claim: Autumn = cinnamon & clove oils;
   Unscented = fragrance-free. **NSF wording applies to Autumn only.**
7. **What should I check before switching table-cleaning supplies?**
   Pain: fear of an inspection finding. Benefit: a printable checklist. Claim: Autumn —
   "NSF-certified" + "suitable for food service environments"; plus the Eco/C cloth wording with
   the mandatory disclaimer.
8. **Should labour minutes count in my cleaning cost?**
   Pain: labour is invisible in a per-item price. Benefit: the true cost per table.
   Claim: third-party — labour is **30–35% of expenses** in casual dining (Worldmetrics 2026);
   a dedicated busser can **halve** reset time (Restaurant Booking System).
